[[144,12,12]] gross code decoder — VU19P floorplan

Placement of the windowed Relay-BP decoder across the 4 SLRs of an AMD VU19P. The 12 colored regions are the 12 syndrome cycles of the W=12 decoding window. Based on Figure 5 of arXiv:2510.21600.

This is IBM's VU19P floorplan for the full [[144,12,12]]-X decoder (paper Figure 5). Aman's project targets VU9P and scopes to CNU + VNU only, so his eventual floorplan will look very different.

Full-decoder utilization (paper Table 1): 2,106,738 LUTs (51.56%) — of which VNUs take 67.2% and CNUs 22.6% — 540,767 FFs (6.62%), 14,052 LUTRAM (1.47%), 29.5 BRAM (1.37%), 58.025 W, 45 user pins. 4 ns FPGA clock, 12 ns decoder cycle, 24 ns per BP iteration (one CNU→VNU + one VNU→CNU decoder cycle).
